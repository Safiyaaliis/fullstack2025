About Redbull

Story behind the brand that "gives you wings"

Red Bull isn’t just a drink – it’s an idea that changed how people think about energy. The story began in the mid-1980s when Austrian entrepreneur Dietrich Mateschitz discovered a popular Thai energy drink called Krating Daeng during a business trip to Asia. He saw potential in bringing the concept to Europe, but he didn’t just want to sell a drink – he wanted to build a lifestyle brand. In 1987, Mateschitz teamed up with the Thai creator Chaleo Yoovidhya and launched Red Bull in Austria. The drink was slightly reformulated to suit Western tastes, and the iconic slogan “Red Bull gives you wings” was born. From the very beginning, Red Bull marketed itself differently – instead of traditional ads, it focused on extreme sports, adventure, and youth culture. The brand quickly became associated with pushing limits, taking risks, and living life to the fullest. Today, Red Bull isn’t just an energy drink; it’s a symbol of motivation, creativity, and adrenaline. With millions of cans sold daily around the world and a strong presence in sports, music, and culture, the brand continues to inspire people to go beyond their comfort zones – just like it did from the start.
